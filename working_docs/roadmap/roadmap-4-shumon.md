# Project Name

**Customer Support through Video Calling**

---

## Application Scope

The solution will be integrated within your "My Prime App."

---

## Customer Journey / Functional Flow

### Step 1

Customer logs into the app and selects the Video Call Support option.

### Step 2

An OTP is sent to the customer's registered mobile number. Upon successful OTP verification, the video call is initiated.

### Step 3

- If an agent is available, the call connects instantly.
- If all agents are busy, the customer is placed in a queue / waiting list.

### Step 4

Once connected, the customer will have access to the following features:

- Face-to-face video support
- Screen sharing
- Chat functionality
- View adjustment
- Full-screen mode

### Step 5

After the support session ends, the customer will receive a feedback and rating option for the call.

---

## Admin Dashboard & Reporting

The admin panel will provide insights on:

- Total number of calls received (daily, weekly, monthly – customizable)
- Call duration
- Customer feedback and ratings
- Average and total waiting time
